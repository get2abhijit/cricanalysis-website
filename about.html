About

Two analysts, one game

cricanalysis.in is an independent site dedicated to cricket analysis, run by Abhijit Deshpande and Aniket Kashikar.

Most cricket analysis follows the biggest teams and leagues. We think the rest of the cricket world deserves the same care. Our focus is on Associate nations and European cricket: their matches, tournaments, players and teams, along with explainers that make the methods behind the numbers easy to follow.

How we work

  • Finding first. Each piece leads with what we found, then shows the evidence.
  • Sources and dates. We name the source of every dataset and the date it covers or was checked.
  • Fact, calculation, opinion. We keep them clearly apart, and label them where it helps.
  • Small samples flagged. Associate records are often short; we say so when a number rests on little evidence.
  • No invented numbers. If we cannot verify a statistic, a result or a player detail, we do not publish it.

Data

Much of our work starts with Cricsheet, which publishes free ball-by-ball data and a register of player identifiers. We add our own player profile data for batting hand and bowling style, and use other public sources where they are needed, always named in the article.

Contact

Spotted an error, or know a player whose details are missing? The easiest way to reach us is via LinkedIn. Corrections are welcome and will be noted in the article.
